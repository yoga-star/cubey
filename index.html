Every morning, somewhere in India

Eggs are everyone's favourite morning protein.
They're also everyone's least favourite morning task.

Cracking. Whisking. Shells in the sink. A pan to scrub. The friction we've all just accepted as the way eggs work.

So we started thinking — what if eggs didn't have to be this hard?

01 — What it is

Eggs, reimagined as a block.

Same eggs you love. None of the friction.

02 — Why it exists

India eats 183+ eggs per person
every year.

That number is still climbing. But every single one of those eggs starts the same way — cracking, whisking, shells in the sink, a pan to scrub.

Cubey was built by someone who grew up watching this every morning. My family has been in the poultry business for years. I know how good Indian eggs are. I also know how much friction comes with cooking them.

Cubey takes the friction out without compromising what makes them eggs.

— Sindhu, founder of Cubey

03 — The friction

Eggs, without the mess.

The old way
  1. Crack 4 eggs.
  2. Whisk in a bowl.
  3. Shells in the sink.
  4. Cook in a pan.
  5. Scrub the pan.
~ 12 min 2 tools to wash
vs
The Cubey way
  1. Open the pack.
  2. Cube on a board.
  3. Drop in your dish.
~ 2 min Zero mess
