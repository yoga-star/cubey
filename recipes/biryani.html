Recipe 05

Cubey Biryani Topper

Turn yesterday's biryani — or a packet of plain rice — into an egg biryani in 10 minutes.

Cubes 8
Time 10 min
Serves 2

You'll need

  • 8 Cubey cubes (one full pack)
  • 2 cups warm cooked biryani (or jeera rice)
  • 1 tbsp ghee
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • 1 small onion, thinly sliced
  • A pinch of saffron in 1 tbsp warm milk
  • A handful of fresh mint leaves
  • Fried onions (birista), to garnish

How to make it

  1. Crisp the onions. Heat ghee in a small pan. Fry the sliced onion till deep golden. Remove and set aside.
  2. Toss the cubes. In the same ghee, add garam masala and Cubey cubes. Toss gently 1–2 minutes until lightly golden.
  3. Saffron. Drizzle the saffron milk over your hot biryani.
  4. Layer. Top with the warm Cubey cubes, fried onions, and mint leaves.
  5. Serve. Bring straight to the table with a side of raita.
Tip

For a deeper flavour, layer the cubes into the biryani pot for 2 minutes on dum (covered, lowest heat) before serving.
