Recipe 03

Cubey Fried Rice

High-heat wok-tossed rice with vegetables and golden cubes — the easy weeknight one-pan.

Cubes 6
Time 8 min
Serves 2

You'll need

  • 6 Cubey cubes (halve them if you want smaller bites)
  • 2 cups cold cooked rice (day-old works best)
  • ½ cup mixed vegetables — carrot, peas, beans
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1 tsp rice vinegar (or lemon juice)
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 spring onion, sliced
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

How to make it

  1. High heat. Heat oil in a wok or wide pan over the highest flame. Add garlic.
  2. Veg first. Add the onion and vegetables. Stir-fry 2–3 minutes until just tender.
  3. Rice in. Add the cold rice. Toss well so every grain coats with oil.
  4. Season. Add soy sauce, vinegar, salt, and pepper. Stir-fry 1 more minute.
  5. Cubey last. Drop in the cubes and toss gently 1 minute — just to warm through.
  6. Top. Finish with spring onion. Eat straight from the pan.
Tip

Add the cubes last so they stay golden and intact. Tossing too early breaks them down.
