Recipe 02

Dal with Cubey Tadka

Comforting toor dal finished with a hot ghee tempering and crisp-edged Cubey cubes.

Cubes 4
Time 10 min
Serves 2

You'll need

  • 4 Cubey cubes (half a pack)
  • 1 cup cooked toor dal (or moong)
  • 2 tbsp ghee
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 2 dry red chilies
  • 4–5 curry leaves
  • 1 small onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • ¼ tsp turmeric
  • Salt, to taste

How to make it

  1. Hot ghee. Heat ghee in a small pan. Add cumin, dry chilies, and curry leaves. Let them crackle.
  2. Aromatics. Add sliced onion and crushed garlic. Sauté 2 minutes until soft.
  3. Toss the cubes. Add Cubey cubes and turmeric. Toss gently for 2 minutes — let the edges crisp.
  4. Pour over dal. Tip the entire tadka over hot dal. Stir very lightly so the cubes stay whole.
  5. Eat. Serve with rice, jeera rice, or roti.
Tip

Skip the cooked dal and pour the tadka straight over rice — Cubey cubes work as a dal-less protein hit too.
