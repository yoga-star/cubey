Cubey Sandwich

A fridge-raid sandwich for hostel kitchens, office lunches, and 4 p.m. hunger.

Cubes 4
Time 5 min
Serves 1

You'll need

  • 4 Cubey cubes, halved or sliced thin
  • 2 slices of bread — sourdough, multigrain, or whatever's around
  • 1 tbsp mayo
  • 1 tsp Dijon (or kasundi)
  • A few slices of cucumber
  • A few slices of tomato
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Chili flakes or hot sauce, optional

How to make it

  1. Toast. Lightly toast both slices of bread.
  2. Spread. Mayo on one slice, Dijon on the other.
  3. Layer. Cucumber and tomato on the mayo side.
  4. Cubey. Top with the Cubey slices. Season with salt, pepper, and chili flakes.
  5. Close. Press gently. Cut diagonal. Eat immediately or wrap for later.
Tip

Slice cubes into 4–5mm slabs instead of cubing them — they layer flatter in a sandwich and cover more bread.
