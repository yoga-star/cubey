Cubey Egg Curry

A weekday egg curry that comes together faster than the rice you'll serve it with.

Cubes 8
Time 15 min
Serves 2

You'll need

  • 8 Cubey cubes (one 200g pack)
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 2 medium tomatoes, pureed
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 green chili, slit
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt, to taste
  • Fresh coriander, to garnish

How to make it

  1. Heat the oil. Sauté the chopped onion till golden — 3–4 minutes.
  2. Aromatics. Add ginger-garlic paste and green chili. Cook for a minute.
  3. Masala. Add the tomato puree and dry spices. Cook 4–5 minutes until oil starts to separate.
  4. Loosen. Add 1 cup water. Bring to a gentle simmer.
  5. Cubey. Drop in the cubes. Simmer 4–5 minutes — they'll absorb the masala without breaking apart.
  6. Finish. Garnish with coriander. Serve hot with rice or roti.
Tip

Cubey holds its shape — don't stir too aggressively. A gentle swirl is all you need.
