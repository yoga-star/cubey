Cubey Butter Masala

Paneer butter masala energy — but with eggs. Cubey cubes simmered in a rich, creamy tomato-butter gravy. Best mopped up with hot butter naan.

Cubes 8
Time 20 min
Serves 2

You'll need

  • 8 Cubey cubes (one 200g pack)
  • 3 medium tomatoes, roughly chopped
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 8 cashews
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp fresh cream (or whole milk)
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp Kashmiri red chili powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp kasuri methi (dried fenugreek)
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • Salt, to taste
  • Fresh coriander, to garnish

How to make it

  1. Soften the base. In a pan, melt 1 tbsp butter. Add chopped tomatoes, onion, cashews, ginger-garlic paste, and ½ cup water. Cover and simmer 8 minutes until everything is pulp.
  2. Blend. Cool slightly, blend into a smooth purée. Strain through a fine sieve for that restaurant silk (optional but worth it).
  3. Tadka. Wipe the pan. Melt the remaining 1 tbsp butter. Add chili powder and coriander powder. Cook 20 seconds — don't burn.
  4. Gravy. Pour the purée back in. Simmer 4 minutes. Stir in cream, sugar, salt, and the kasuri methi (crushed between your palms first).
  5. Cubey in. Drop in the cubes. Simmer gently 3–4 minutes — they'll soak up the colour without breaking.
  6. Finish. Sprinkle garam masala and fresh coriander. Serve hot with butter naan, jeera rice, or both.
Tip

Kashmiri chili powder gives the gravy its signature red without the heat. If yours isn't red enough, a teaspoon of beetroot juice fixes it instantly.
