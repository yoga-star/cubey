Cubey Tikka

Yogurt-marinated, pan-seared, char-edged Cubey cubes. Eat them off a stick with mint chutney, or roll them into a kathi.

Cubes 8
Time 15 min
Serves 2

You'll need

  • 8 Cubey cubes (one 200g pack)
  • 3 tbsp thick curd / Greek yogurt
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp Kashmiri red chili powder
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • ½ tsp chaat masala (+ extra to finish)
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp oil (mustard or sunflower)
  • Salt, to taste
  • Fresh coriander + a lemon wedge, to serve
  • Sliced raw red onion + mint chutney, to serve

How to make it

  1. Marinate. In a bowl, whisk curd, ginger-garlic paste, all the spices (except the extra chaat masala), lemon juice, and salt. Add the Cubey cubes, toss gently, rest 10 minutes (or skip if you're hungry).
  2. Hot pan. Heat oil in a heavy pan over medium-high. Wait until it shimmers.
  3. Sear. Lift the cubes out of the marinade and place them in the pan with a little space between each one. Sear 1–2 minutes per side until edges char and the marinade caramelises.
  4. Finish. Pull the cubes off, sprinkle with the extra chaat masala and fresh coriander. Squeeze over the lemon wedge.
  5. Serve. Plate with sliced raw onion, mint chutney, and an extra wedge of lemon. Eat with toothpicks or roll into a paratha for a kathi.
Tip

For tandoor-grade char without a tandoor: thread the marinated cubes onto skewers and finish under a hot oven grill for 1–2 minutes per side.
