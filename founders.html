Why it exists

Here's how Cubey started.

Sindhu grew up in a poultry family. Watched India cook eggs the hard way every single morning. Got tired of it. Built a cube.

Mounika looked at that cube, looked at the market, and said "okay I'll take it from here."

Sindhu broke the problem. Mounika is making sure it never breaks again.

One family has chickens.
The other one has spreadsheets.
Both are equally dangerous.

— Sindhu & Mounika, founders

Disclaimer: No chickens were consulted in the making of this company.
